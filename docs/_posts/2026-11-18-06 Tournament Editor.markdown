**Every aspect of the generated tournament structure is easily configurable, including:**

- Buyins, Rebuys, Addons,
- Blinds schedule (edit the small blind and the big blind will auto-populate with 2x the small blind, although you can over-ride it),
- Chips available (typical chip colors and denominations will be pre-populated, but edit to your heart's content, including the color, denomination, text color, edge spot color and even the number of edge spots), and
- Payout calculation parameters
