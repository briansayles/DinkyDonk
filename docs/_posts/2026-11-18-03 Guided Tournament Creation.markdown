Creating a **good blinds schedule** is critical to a poker tournament's success. This program helps by creating a new, **logical tournament structure** for you in seconds. Just enter

- Your smallest chip denomination,
- The number of players you expect, and
- Your desired tournament length

Most other apps just use math to create a blinds schedule, so they end up with weird levels that nobody wants, like 32/64. We use a **curated list of blinds** as our starting point instead, so you'll get a much better structure by default.
  
Unless it's just impossible based on your inputs, the generated blinds schedule will gradually increase over time. If your inputs won't allow for that, **certain** levels will be deleted to get you the best structure possible.
