Create a new, **logical tournament structure** in seconds, by entering 
  1. Your smallest chip denomination,
  2. The number of players you expect, and
  3. Your desired tournament length

  Unlike many other apps, this one uses a curated list of blinds as its starting point, so you won't see levels like 32/64 or 48/96. 
  
  The curated list will create a gradually increasing structure, if possible based on your inputs. If it can't, it will smartly remove certain levels to try to produce a tournament that meets your specifications.
