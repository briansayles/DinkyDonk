Shows the lowest 4 chips, by denomination, in play 

When a chip with can be colored-up after the current level, it will fade in and out as a gentle reminder

Unnecessary chips (those that should be colored-up) drift off the screen at the end of their last level

Larger chips drift in as smaller ones drift off
